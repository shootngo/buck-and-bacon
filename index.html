Safety & sources

Buck and Bacon is a home sausage scaler for Frank Mulkey. It is not USDA inspection, not a substitute for your cure product’s label, and not medical or legal advice.

Cure #1

Cure amounts are computed, never copied from recipes or videos. The app assumes Cure #1 (Prague Powder #1 / Instacure #1) at 2.5 g per kg of total meat, which is the same rate as 1 oz per 25 lb (~156 ppm sodium nitrite when the product is 6.25% nitrite).

Confirm against the jar in your hand. Some pink salts are a different concentration. Do not use Cure #2 for these cooked/smoked sausages. Weigh cure on a gram scale.

DH Custom Sausage notes

Seasoning formulas follow Duncan Henry’s public method: ingredients as grams per kilogram of meat so the math stays in tenths. Shop videos are used for style (garlic kielbasa, snack sticks, breakfast sage), not for nitrite numbers.
